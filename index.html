Seat positions

Select a marker or position below. Filled markers mean installed; outlined markers mean available. Mutually excluded seats are hidden on the plan. Amber dashed markers mean installed seats folded up for a basket. Marker positions show seat-arm references, not measured seat outlines.

Shared seating spaces: C5 or P13/P14 · C6 or P7/P8. A passenger pair can be installed together. Occupants must be cleared before replacing their seats.
